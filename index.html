FBR Tax Guides & Frequently Asked Questions

Search our curated repository for instant answers regarding Active Taxpayer List (ATL) rules, withholding tax rates, and legal compliance.

An Active Filer is an individual or entity whose name appears on the FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL) after regularly filing annual income tax returns. Filers enjoy standard or reduced withholding tax rates (e.g. 15% on bank profit, lower property transfer duty). A Non-Filer pays up to 100% to 200% higher tax penalties on vehicle purchase, property sales, dividend payments, and cash transactions.
Under the Income Tax Ordinance, IT services and software export earnings brought into Pakistan through formal banking channels are subject to a concessional final withholding tax rate of 0.25%, provided the taxpayer is registered with PSEB (Pakistan Software Export Board) and files annual returns.
If you fail to file your tax return by the due date, your name will be removed from the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) on March 1st. To rejoin the ATL, late filers must pay a statutory surcharge (PKR 1,000 for salaried individuals, PKR 10,000 for sole proprietors/businesses, and PKR 20,000 for companies) along with late filing penalties under Section 182.
Company registration requires: 1) Proposed company name for SECP availability, 2) CNIC/Passport copies of directors and shareholders, 3) Registered office address proof, 4) Memorandum & Articles of Association (MOA/AOA), and 5) Paid-up capital details.
